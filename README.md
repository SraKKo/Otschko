#Otshcko!

Otshcko! is a fast-paced, time-pressured take on classic blackjack, designed for short, intense play sessions. 
Each round is played against a countdown timer. Hesitate too long and the round ends whether you’re ready or not. The pressure transforms familiar blackjack rules into something sharper and more aggressive.
Playable on Itch.io [Here!](https://srakko.itch.io/otshcko)
